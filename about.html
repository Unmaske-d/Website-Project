ABOUT

Why Experience
Exchange?

Everyday situations can become very different depending on the circumstances surrounding them.

THE PROBLEM

The same task
isn't always the same experience.

Getting somewhere, finding information or completing an everyday task can involve barriers that aren't immediately visible to other people.

Accessibility, unclear information and limited financial resources can all affect which choices someone has available.

This can make an ordinary journey more complicated, time-consuming or restrictive.

OUR APPROACH

Make the
invisible visible.

01

Start with something familiar

The scenarios are based around everyday goals that are easy to understand.

02

Introduce a barrier

A circumstance changes what options are available during the journey.

03

Show the consequences

Different choices can affect factors such as time, money or the route itself.

04

Encourage reflection

The experience gives visitors an opportunity to think about barriers they might otherwise overlook.

A NOTE ON PERSPECTIVE

A simplified experience cannot represent a whole life.

Experience Exchange is not intended to simulate what it is like to live with a particular circumstance.

Real experiences are personal and much more complex than an interactive scenario can represent. Two people facing a similar barrier may have completely different experiences.

Instead, this project uses simplified situations to encourage awareness of how circumstances can influence everyday choices.

THE PROJECT

Designed to make
you think twice.

Experience Exchange is a UX project exploring how interactive design can communicate a societal issue through experience rather than explanation alone.
