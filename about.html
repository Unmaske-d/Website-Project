ABOUT EXPERIENCE EXCHANGE

Some barriers are invisible until you encounter them.

Experience Exchange is an interactive concept designed to encourage people to think about how circumstances can influence everyday tasks.

OUR APPROACH

Not a simulation.
A perspective.

A short interactive experience cannot reproduce another person's lived experience. Instead, Experience Exchange focuses on something simpler: showing how the same goal can involve different obstacles.

By making those differences visible, we hope to encourage curiosity about experiences that might otherwise remain unnoticed.
