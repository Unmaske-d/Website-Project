HOW IT WORKS

Same goal.
Different circumstances.

Experience Exchange uses interactive scenarios to explore how everyday barriers can change an otherwise ordinary journey.

THE PROCESS

Four steps.

01

Choose a situation

Start with a familiar everyday goal, such as travelling to a destination.

02

Receive circumstances

A circumstance is introduced at the beginning of your journey and can affect the choices available to you.

03

Navigate

Make decisions as you encounter different situations along the way.

04

Reflect

Consider how the circumstances you encountered changed your route, choices or experience.

YOUR CHOICES

There isn't always a right answer.

The scenarios are not designed as tests. Instead, your choices can involve different trade-offs.

A faster option might cost more. A cheaper route might take longer. Another option might only become available because of the circumstances you're facing.

The goal is not to find the perfect route. It is to explore how circumstances can change the choices available to someone.

A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE

An experience,
not a simulation.

01

It cannot tell the whole story

A short interactive scenario cannot reproduce someone's real-life experiences or circumstances.

02

It can make barriers visible

Instead, Experience Exchange uses simplified situations to draw attention to barriers that can otherwise be easy to overlook.

03

Context matters

The same situation can lead to different choices depending on someone's circumstances and resources.

04

Think beyond the journey

The experience is meant to encourage reflection about accessibility, information and financial barriers in everyday life.
