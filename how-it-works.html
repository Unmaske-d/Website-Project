HOW IT WORKS

Same goal.
Different circumstances.

Experience Exchange uses interactive scenarios to explore how everyday barriers can change an otherwise ordinary journey.

THE PROCESS

Four steps.

01

Choose a situation

Start with a familiar everyday goal that most people can recognise.

02

Receive circumstances

Your circumstances are selected at the beginning of the journey, changing what options may be available.

03

Navigate

Make decisions as you encounter different situations. Your earlier choices can affect what happens later.

04

Reflect

Look back at your journey and consider how your circumstances affected the choices you had.

YOUR CHOICES

There isn't always
a right answer.

The experiences are not designed as quizzes. You aren't being tested on whether you can find the "correct" option.

Some choices may save time but cost money. Others may be cheaper but take longer, or may introduce a different problem later in the journey.

The goal is to explore the choices and trade-offs that circumstances can create.

A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE

An experience,
not a simulation.

Experience Exchange cannot reproduce another person's lived experience. Everyone's circumstances and experiences are different.

Instead, these scenarios are designed to make everyday barriers more visible and encourage you to think about how circumstances can affect the choices available to different people.
