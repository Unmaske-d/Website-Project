THE IDEA

One goal.
Many possible paths.

An everyday task can look completely different depending on someone's circumstances. A route that seems simple at first can become more difficult when information, accessibility, time or money become barriers.

Experience Exchange lets you explore these differences through interactive everyday scenarios. Instead of simply telling you about a barrier, it lets you encounter choices and trade-offs along the way.
